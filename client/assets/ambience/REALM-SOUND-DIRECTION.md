# Sound direction — Reino de Bloodmoon

The open world has its own evolving ambience and never uses the title/menu track. Four crossfading beds rotate roughly every 1½–3 minutes: cold snowfall with a distant temple resonance; a haunted keep under winter wind; a rain-soaked frontier with February-storm texture; and an eclipse hush blending snowfall, haunted rooms, and whispers. Slow playback drift keeps the loops from feeling mechanical. Bed layers stay restrained (roughly 0.10–0.16 each) so exploration, combat, and interface cues remain clear.

Storms change the world as well as the sound: lightning briefly exposes the map, then a delayed crack, deep boom, or rolling storm impact arrives from a varied pool. Rare split thunder tails make large strikes feel distant and physical. The existing Arena score and battle mix stay unchanged; returning to the title or refuge restores its original music scene.

All eleven files in the incoming `ambiente_sfx` drop feed the soundscape: rainstorm, deep boom, creepy ambience, February storm, howling winter storm, temple noise, cold snowfall, haunted-house ambience, rolling storm, and thunder strike. The two cold-snowfall files have identical SHA-256 content, so the game serves one copy. The new long beds remain lazy-loaded and are not part of the initial game download.
